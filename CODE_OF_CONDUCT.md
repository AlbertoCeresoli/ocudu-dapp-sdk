# Code of conduct

Project participants must communicate professionally and respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect a productive technical community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion, subject to legal and safety obligations.
